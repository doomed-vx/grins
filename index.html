The Origin

Born from loneliness. Shaped by rejection. Trapped behind a smile that never fades.

Grin is not merely a horror character. Grin is the embodiment of human duality — the happiness we display versus the sorrow we bury deep.

Once a small, bald, crying creature reaching out for comfort in the dark, Grin learned the cruel lesson that the world does not embrace tears. So a permanent smile was forged — an armor that backfired into something far more terrifying.

The wider the smile stretches, the deeper the wound beneath. The whiter the face glows, the darker the void inside. Grin does not kill. Grin watches. And in that silent stare, you see yourself.
